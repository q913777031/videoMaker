## 参考资料

## 事实核查与表述说明

- 共同体感觉（Gemeinschaftsgefühl，英译 social interest）是阿德勒晚期的核心概念，阿德勒学派以它作为心理健康的衡量标准（Ansbacher 1968）。
- 第 6 镜为 What Life Should Mean to You 原句大意：“It is the individual who is not interested in his fellow men who has the greatest difficulties in life and provides the greatest injury to others.”
- 第 11 镜为第 1 章原句大意：“Life means—to be interested in my fellow men, to be part of the whole, to contribute my share to the welfare of mankind.”
- “贡献感”（幸福就是贡献感）是《被讨厌的勇气》第五夜的表述，第 7 镜标注“岸见一郎阐释”。
